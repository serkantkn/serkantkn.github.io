PocketFarm closed beta

Before PocketFarm goes out on Google Play it goes through a closed test. Join it and you install the game from Play, new versions arrive by themselves, and we shape Play's first release together.

Android 9+phone and tablet
Freeno ads and no in-app purchases
14 daysGoogle's rule: at least 12 people keep it installed for 14 days

How to join

All you need is a Google account: the one you use on the Play Store. Three steps, a few minutes.

  1. Join the PocketFarm beta group

    Press "Join group" on Google Groups. The account that joins goes on the testers' list by itself. The group is not a mailing list; you get no email, and only I see who is in it.

    Join the group →

  2. Become a tester

    On Play's opt-in page, with the account you joined the group with, press "Become a tester". If you have only just joined, the page may say "app not found" for a few minutes; try again a little later.

    Become a tester →

  3. Install from the Play Store and play

    PocketFarm shows on the Play Store as "early access". Install it and keep it: testers need to keep it installed for 14 days. Opening it now and then to check on your farm is enough; how much you play is up to you.

    Open on the Play Store →

If you cannot join the group

Work and school accounts sometimes cannot join Google Groups. Then fill in the form below; I add your address to the testers' list by hand and send you the opt-in link (usually within a day or two). Then steps 2 and 3 above.

Which devices will you try it on?

Good to know

  • If you installed it from GitHub: the Play version carries the same signature; install it from Play without uninstalling PocketFarm, and your farm stays as it is. From then on updates come from Play.
  • Your farm comes along: sign in with Google or Play Games in Settings if you like, and your farm follows you across phone, tablet, iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Feedback: for bugs and ideas, open an issue on GitHub or send private feedback from the game's page on the Play Store.
  • Leaving the beta: press "Leave the program" on the opt-in page; you can leave the group too. The game and your farm stay.
  • iPhone and iPad: PocketFarm is already out on the App Store; get it free there.

Your sign-up details

If you join the group, Google keeps your membership; you can leave the group whenever you like. If you fill in the form, your name, address and the devices you chose are kept in a Google Form (on Google's servers). They are used only to add you to the testers' list on Google Play and to write to you about the beta; they are shared with nobody and deleted when the beta ends. To have them deleted sooner, write to pocketvillesupport@gmail.com. For the game itself, see the privacy policy.
